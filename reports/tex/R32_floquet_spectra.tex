\begin{abstract}
We diagonalise the one-period Floquet operator $U$ of all sixteen unitary rules with the Hadamard gate, for $N=6\ldots14$
sites with open boundaries. For every eigenstate we record the quasienergy, the half-chain entanglement and the gap ratio.
All symmetries are resolved first: Krylov sectors, the blocks of the algebra generated by the local gates (which also
captures quantum fragmentation and hidden internal symmetries), and the spatial symmetry. The spatial symmetry is the
reflection for odd $N$ and, for even $N$, the half step $\Omega=RL_A$ with $\Omega^2=U$.

Because $U$ is real orthogonal and a product of two reflections, the chaotic reference is the $\beta=1$ value
$\langle r\rangle=0.5307$, computed on the half spectrum. We verified this against random reflections.

The sixteen rules sort by the number $k$ of neighbour patterns that fire the gate:
\begin{itemize}
\item $k=3$ (54, 57, 99, 147): the only chaotic rules. $\langle r\rangle=0.535$ for 57 and 99, and $0.526$ for 147, the
  latter only after symmetry resolution (0.424 without). Each carries an $N$-independent number of exact eigenstates of
  every individual gate: two or four product states, and for 54 a single entangled area-law state.
\item $k=1$: exponentially fragmented, with Poisson statistics inside, as expected for tensor products of independent rooms.
\item $k=2$ XOR (150, 105): arithmetic spectra. For 150 the count of distinct quasienergies reproduces R8's $3^m$ law
  exactly up to $N=14$.
\item $k=2$ one-sided: a boundary-driven block tower, with $\langle r\rangle$ near Poisson but drifting upward.
\item $k=0,4$: trivial.
\end{itemize}
The literature on these diagnostics for Floquet operators is reviewed, with every reference checked online.
\end{abstract}
